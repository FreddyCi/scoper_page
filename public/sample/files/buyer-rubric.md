# Buyer Evaluation Rubric — AMI Smart Metering Program

**Employer / Utility context:** State-owned electricity distribution utility running a
multi-year Advanced Metering Infrastructure (AMI) rollout. Consortium bids permitted
(max 3 members). This rubric reflects our internal must-haves for scoring bidder
responses against the RFP (Model-RfP-Final_0).

## Mandatory (deal-breaker if missing)

- **Quality certification** — ISO 9001:2008 *or* CMMI Level 3 (minimum), **plus**
  ISO 14001 **and** ISO 27001, all current as of tender publication date (RFP
  Section 4.3, QR Table Item 2).
- **Technical experience** — cumulative 10,000+ communication endpoints deployed;
  NIC/communication module integrated with meters from at least 3 manufacturers in
  India up to HES/MDMS (RFP Section 4.3, QR Table Item 1).
- **Financial standing** — positive net worth in the best 3 of the last 5 financial
  years; Minimum Average Annual Turnover (MAAT) at or above the published threshold
  (RFP Section 4.3, QR Table Item D).
- **Insurance** — full insurance of Goods against loss/damage during manufacture,
  transport, storage, and delivery, in place (not "pending") at time of bid
  (Clause 6.28).
- **Indemnification** — uncapped or high-cap acceptance of Clause 6.33 as drafted;
  requests to cap aggregate indemnity liability are a flag, not an automatic fail,
  but should be scored down.
- **Termination** — acceptance of [Employer]'s right to terminate for convenience on
  90 days' notice (Clause 6.40) without a compensation rider.

## Nice-to-have (score up, don't disqualify)

- Fixed-fee / milestone-based pricing over time-and-materials (T&M), per the
  Payment Schedule structure at Clause 6.19.
- CGL / professional indemnity coverage above and beyond the base Clause 6.28
  transit/storage insurance (e.g., USD 2M+ per occurrence).
- SLA commitment of 99.9% monthly-audited availability (Clause 6.44) rather than
  lower thresholds or longer audit cycles.
- In-India data hosting with no offshore DR ambiguity.
- Consortium structure that cleanly satisfies Clause 4.3.2 without qualification.

## Scoring guidance

| Signal in bidder response | Treatment |
|---|---|
| Certification "in progress" / "expected next quarter" | Gap — does not meet QR as drafted |
| Insurance "pending renewal" or "upon award" | Gap — Clause 6.28 requires cover in place |
| T&M pricing with automatic annual uplift, no change-order gate | Gap vs Clause 6.38 process |
| Requested cap on indemnity (Clause 6.33) | Flag — negotiable but score down |
| Request to remove/compensate termination-for-convenience (Clause 6.40) | Flag — material deviation |
| Turnover requested on "consolidated group" basis | Flag — verify against RFP's bidder definition |

## How to use this file

Attach as supporting context in Evaluation Setup. It is not scored as a bidder
document — it steers which RFP clauses the qualification engine should treat as
mandatory vs. nice-to-have when scanning bidder PDFs for certifications, insurance,
pricing model, and liability/termination language.
